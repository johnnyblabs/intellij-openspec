Usage: openspec workset [options] [command]

Compose, keep, and open personal working views (purely local)

Options:
  -h, --help               display help for command

Commands:
  create [options] [name]  Compose and save a named working view of folders you
                           choose
  list|ls [options]        Show saved worksets with their members
  open [options] <name>    Open a saved workset in your tool (editor window or
                           agent session)
  remove [options] <name>  Delete a saved workset (member folders are never
                           touched)
